Scale and arpeggio practice sheets, generated for any key

Scale Workshop turns a scale into practice material. Pick a root and a scale, tick the patterns you want, and every pattern comes back engraved as sheet music you can play back, download as a MIDI file, save as SVG, or print as a PDF practice sheet. It runs entirely in your browser — nothing is uploaded, nothing is installed, and it keeps working offline.

Scales

38 scales: major and its modes (ionian, dorian, phrygian, lydian, mixolydian, aeolian, locrian), harmonic and melodic minor and their modes, major and minor pentatonics, blues scales, the bebop scales, the symmetric scales (whole tone, both diminished, augmented, chromatic), plus Hungarian minor, the Neapolitans, double harmonic, hirajoshi, kumoi and in-sen.

Patterns

30 patterns: the straight scale; broken 3rds, 4ths, 5ths, 6ths, 7ths and octaves; groupings such as 1-2-3-4, 1-2-3-5, 1-3-2-4, turns, five-in-a-row and zig-zags; arpeggios from triads and sevenths out to 9ths, 11ths and 13ths, stacked on every scale degree; chromatic approach notes and enclosures; expanding intervals, tonic pedal and random permutations.

Keys, modes and rhythm

Run an exercise from every degree of its scale, and take it round the cycle of fourths or fifths, half steps, whole steps, minor or major thirds — up to all twelve keys in one sheet. One to three octaves, ascending, descending or both, treble or bass clef, quarter notes through sixteenth-note sextuplets, 40 to 240 bpm.
